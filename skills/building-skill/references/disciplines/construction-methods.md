# Construction Methods by Region and System

Purpose: know HOW buildings are physically built in each place so sections, details and schedules match local practice. Code numbers live in `references/countries/*.md`. Dimensions marked (RoT) are rules of thumb for concept drawings, not design values. "⚠ verify" = not confirmed from a primary source; check with local engineer or manufacturer.

Conventions: o.c. = on centre; RC = reinforced concrete; CMU = concrete masonry unit; EPS/XPS = expanded/extruded polystyrene; PIR = polyisocyanurate; DPC/DPM = damp-proof course/membrane; ETICS = External Thermal Insulation Composite System (EIFS in US).

## 1. Structural systems overview

| System | Where dominant | Typical limits (RoT) | Pros | Cons | Shows on drawings as |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light wood frame, platform | USA, Canada, Japan (2x4), Australia/NZ, Nordics (variant) | 1-4 storeys residential; 5-6 with podium/ mid-rise rules; studs 2x4/2x6; joists span 3.5-5.5 m | Fast, cheap, light, easy MEP in cavities | Fire/moisture/termite care, low mass | Repetitive stud lines, double top plate, rim joist, sheathing line, floor-by-floor break |
| Light wood frame, balloon | Historic N. America, some Nordic | Legacy only; fire-stop issue | Long continuous studs | Fire spread in cavity | Studs running through floors, ledger/ribbon board |
| Cold-formed steel (CFS) | Australia, USA commercial/ mid-rise infill, UK/Europe SFS/LGSF, Gulf modular | Studs 90-150 mm; 1-6 storeys | Non-combustible, dimensionally stable | Thermal bridging; needs external insulation | Thin C-sections with track top/bottom; screw symbols; strapping bracing |
| CLT / mass timber | Austria, Germany, Switzerland, Nordics, UK, Canada, USA (growing), Japan | Floors 5-ply 175-200 mm spans ~5-6 m (RoT, manufacturer tables govern); buildings 8-18+ storeys | Prefab speed, low carbon, exposed finish | Fire char design, moisture in erection, acoustic layers needed | Solid panel hatch (cross-grain), steel angle brackets/screws at joints, topping + acoustic layer above |
| Timber post-and-beam / heavy timber | Japan (traditional), alpine, N. America (timber frame), Germany (Fachwerk), glulam halls | Spans 4-12 m glulam beams; large halls 20-40 m | Open plan, visible frame | Infill/bracing detailing, cost | Discrete posts and beams on grid, infill walls drawn separately, bracing lines |
| Load-bearing masonry (brick/block) | UK low-rise, Germany, Austria, Italy, Spain, Middle East older, India (small), Africa | Walls 200-365 mm; 1-5 storeys; cross-walls ~5 m apart | Mass, durable, no frame | Slower, limited openings, seismic weakness unreinforced | Thick solid walls carrying slab bearing; lintels over every opening; no columns |
| Confined masonry | Chile, Colombia, Peru, Mexico, Iran, Indonesia, Italy/Slovenia/Serbia origins | 1-4 storeys; walls 120-200 mm | Seismic performance with cheap materials | Needs strict tie-column/beam discipline | Masonry panels framed by small RC tie-columns (~150-200 mm) and tie-beams at every slab; toothed wall-to-column edge |
| RC frame + masonry infill | Gulf, Egypt, Levant, Turkey, India, Pakistan, SE Asia, Latin America, Africa urban, S. Europe | Residential to ~8 storeys by engineer-detailed frame; spans 4-6.5 m; slab 150-250 mm | Flexible, ubiquitous labour skill | Infill not structural but affects seismic behaviour; cracking at frame/infill joint | Column grid, beams (shown dashed or in plan with thick lines), infill block walls as separate hatch, column-wall flush faces |
| RC flat slab (+ drop panels/ capitals) | Gulf towers, Egypt, commercial blocks worldwide | Span 6-9 m; thickness ~ L/30 to L/35 (RoT, reinforced), 200-300 mm | Flat soffit, fast formwork, easy services | Punching shear, deflection | No beams, column heads/drop panels, slab thickness callout, shear-stud rails |
| RC ribbed / hollow-block (hourdi, voided) slab | Egypt, Gulf, Italy, France, Spain, Turkey, N. Africa | Spans 4-7 m; 200-300 mm total | Light, lower concrete | Fire/holes through ribs, vibration | Rib lines with infill blocks (hollow clay/ polystyrene) hatch, solid bands at supports |
| Precast concrete (hollow-core, double-tee, wall panels, volumetric) | Gulf villas/ labour camps, Europe, USA parking/ warehouses, SE Asia (Singapore, HK PPVC) | Hollow-core 150-400 mm, spans 5-15 m; double-tee up to ~20+ m | Speed, quality | Joints, crane access, connection detailing | Panel mark numbers, grouted joints, bearing seats, topping screed line |
| Structural steel | Commercial/ industrial worldwide; Gulf towers/ warehouses; USA offices | Beams L/20-L/25 depth (RoT); spans 6-15 m; 3+ storeys common | Long spans, fast | Fireproofing, corrosion, cost | Grid with section tags (W/UB/UC/IPE/HEA), moment/ simple connections, composite deck with studs |
| ICF (insulated concrete form) | USA, Canada, Australia, Gulf (limited), Europe niche | Walls 150-300 mm core + 2x50-65 mm EPS; 1-4 storeys homes | Airtight, thermal mass + insulation | Cost, detailing of openings | Foam-concrete-foam wall sandwich hatch; horizontal/vertical rebar notes |
| AAC (autoclaved aerated concrete) block | Germany (Ytong/Porenbeton), Poland, Czech, India, Gulf, Egypt, Africa, SE Asia | Walls 100-365 mm; load-bearing up to ~3-5 storeys with engineer; thin-bed mortar 2-3 mm | Light, insulating, easy cutting | Moisture absorption, needs render/ cladding | Block course with thin joints; often unhatched/ light stipple; U-block lintels |
| Rammed earth / adobe / cob | Africa, Middle East heritage, Andes, SW USA, Australia (rammed earth, design-led), Yemen | Walls 300-600 mm; 1-3 storeys | Thermal mass, local material | Water erosion, seismic (unreinforced), low-rise only | Thick walls, overhangs, plinth/ stone base above splash zone, lime/ earth render |

Quick selection heuristics (RoT):
- Seismic high + cheap + 1-4 storeys: confined masonry or RC frame with ductile detailing.
- Cold climate + insulation priority + speed: timber frame/ CLT/ SIP.
- Hot-arid + mass + cheap labour: RC frame + thermal block, precast, or load-bearing block villas.
- Long-span commercial: steel + composite deck, or RC post-tensioned flat slab.

## 2. By region / country

For each: residential typical, commercial typical, signature details to draw.

### 2.1 USA / Canada
Residential
- Platform-framed wood: 2x4 @ 16" o.c. (older/ interior) or 2x6 @ 16"/24" o.c. exterior (energy codes, thicker insulation); double top plate, single bottom plate, headers over openings, king+jack studs.
- Floor: I-joists or dimensional lumber 9.5-14", 16"/19.2"/24" o.c.; 3/4" T&G OSB/plywood subfloor, glued + nailed; rim board at perimeter.
- Roof: prefabricated wood trusses @ 24" o.c. (RoT), 5/12-8/12 pitch typical, OSB 7/16-5/8" sheathing, felt/ synthetic underlayment, asphalt shingles; ridge + soffit vents or sealed attic.
- Wall sheathing: OSB or plywood 7/16"; weather-resistive barrier (house wrap); rainscreen increasingly (furring); cladding vinyl, fibre-cement, wood, brick veneer (1" air gap, ties, weeps), stucco (Southwest, 3-coat or 1-coat on lath).
- Interior: 1/2" gypsum board, 5/8" Type X at garage/ fire separations; interior partitions 2x4 @ 16"; insulation batt/ spray foam/ blown cellulose.
- Foundation by climate: basement (cold, frost depth below footing; concrete or CMU wall 8"/10" on 16-24" wide footing) / crawlspace / slab-on-grade (south, with thickened edge). Footing bottom must be below frost depth (local figure from jurisdiction; varies from ~0 in Gulf Coast to 4+ ft northern US and 1.2-2.4 m Canada). ⚠ verify per municipality.
- Slab-on-grade: 4" concrete, 6-mil poly vapour retarder over 4" compacted gravel, WWF/ rebar, sub-slab insulation in cold zones.
- Basement: footing + stem wall (poured 8-10" or CMU), damp-proofing/ waterproofing membrane, drainage board, perimeter drain tile to sump, interior stud wall with insulation.
Commercial
- Steel frame + composite metal deck (3" deck + 2.5-3.5" concrete) with shear studs; or tilt-up concrete panels (retail/ warehouse), or CMU bearing walls + steel joists / open-web bar joists + metal roof deck.
- Cold-formed steel stud exterior walls + exterior gypsum sheathing + air/vapour barrier + continuous insulation + brick/ metal panel/ EIFS.
- Mid-rise: Type III/ V wood over concrete podium (5 over 1/2) ⚠ verify local height limits; CLT/ mass timber rising.
- Curtain wall: aluminium unitised or stick; spandrel with insulation.
Signature drawing details: wall section from footing to ridge, "SOG" (slab on grade), "CJ" control joints, anchor bolts at sill plate, hold-downs, shear-wall nailing schedule, truss layout plan, building-envelope layers listed top-down on wall tag.

### 2.2 United Kingdom (and Ireland, similar)
Residential
- Strip or trench-fill foundations (RoT: ~600 mm wide for external walls, 450 mm internal bearing; minimum ~1.0 m depth, deeper in clay/ near trees, to be confirmed by engineer/ NHBC chapter 4.2). Raft in poor soils.
- Ground floor: either ground-bearing slab (150 mm concrete on DPM + insulation) or suspended beam-and-block (precast inverted-T beams, dense/ lightweight infill blocks, insulation + screed above, ventilated void with air bricks) or suspended timber joists on sleeper walls (older).
- Walls: masonry cavity wall — 102.5 mm outer brick leaf, 100-150 mm cavity (partial-fill insulation or full-fill), 100 mm inner blockwork (dense or lightweight aircrete, ≥~3.6-7.3 N/mm² block), plaster/ drylining; wall ties at ~900x450 mm (RoT); DPC 150 mm above ground; cavity trays + weep holes over openings; steel/ concrete lintels.
- Timber frame (Scotland ~dominant, rest increasing): 140-190 mm studs, OSB, breather membrane, 50 mm cavity, brick outer leaf with ties to frame.
- Roof: cut-and-pitch or trussed rafters @ 600 mm o.c., 30-45° pitch, concrete/ clay tiles or slate on battens + breathable underlay; 270-400 mm loft insulation at ceiling level (cold roof); gable end walls with restraint straps.
- Windows PVC-U/ aluminium, sealed double/ triple glazed; cavity closer at reveals; sills with cavity tray.
Commercial
- Steel frame (S355) + composite deck, RC frame in-situ with flat slab, or precast concrete; cladding as rainscreen (rainscreen panels on rail system, mineral wool, SFS or blockwork backup); raised access floors 150-600 mm; suspended ceilings.
- Brick slip cladding, terracotta, aluminium panel systems; stairs/ cores in RC.
Signature details: cavity closer/ DPC/ cavity tray symbols, "air brick", "movement joint every 6-12 m in clay brick" (RoT, 12 m clay; ~6 m calcium silicate/ concrete) ⚠ verify, thermal bridge junction details (lintel, ground floor edge, window reveal) required for energy modelling.

### 2.3 Germany / Austria / Switzerland
Residential
- Monolithic masonry: Poroton/ hollow clay (Hochlochziegel) 36.5-49 cm filled with perlite/ mineral wool, plastered (lime-cement or gypsum internal, mineral/ silicone resin external).
- Composite masonry: Kalksandstein (calcium silicate) 17.5-24 cm + ETICS (WDVS) 14-24 cm EPS/ mineral wool/ wood fibre + thin render 5-8 mm.
- AAC (Ytong/ Porenbeton) 30-42.5 cm monolithic, thin-bed mortar.
- Prefab timber-frame houses (Fertighaus) dominant in southern Germany/ Austria: ~160-300 mm frame with insulation, wood-fibre or gypsum-fibre boards, ETICS or timber/ plaster rainscreen; delivered as panels with windows, installed in 1-2 days.
- Ground floor/ basement: Bodenplatte (RC raft 20-30 cm, WU-Beton waterproof concrete "white tank" or bitumen "black tank") on frost-proof footing or full basement (Keller); floor build-up: slab / impact-sound + thermal insulation (EPS/ PU) / Estrich (cement or anhydrite screed 50-65 mm with underfloor heating pipes) / finish.
- Roof: Satteldach with timber purlin or rafter roof, Zwischensparrendämmung (between-rafter) 160-260 mm + under-rafter vapour control layer (variable Dampfbremse) + plasterboard; tile cladding on battens; or flat roof with inverted/ warm design.
- Floors: RC slab with Estrich; timber beam ceilings in older houses.
Commercial
- RC frame with flat slab or beam-slab, Halbfertigteile (precast half-slabs with in-situ topping) very common; facade as ETICS, aluminium curtain wall, clinker brick, Sandwich precast panels in industrial.
- Strong prefab: Filigrandecke (lattice girder semi-precast slab), Elementwände.
Signature details: Sockeldetail (plinth with Perimeterdämmung XPS below ground), thermal-break supports at balconies (Isokorb type), roller shutter box (Rollladenkasten) in window head, lime-gypsum internal plaster 10-15 mm, wall thickness total on plans including ETICS (e.g. 17.5 + 16 + render). Heating via underfloor and/or radiators, heat pump rising.

### 2.4 Nordics (Sweden, Norway, Finland, Denmark)
- Timber frame dominant for houses (>80 % single-family in Sweden/ Norway/ Finland ⚠ verify exact figures): 2x4/2x6 studs @ 600 mm o.c. (note 600 not 16"), mineral wool, plastic/ smart vapour barrier on warm side, services cavity 45 mm inside the barrier, plasterboard/ panel; outside: wind barrier board, 22-25 mm ventilated cavity (battens), vertical timber cladding (spruce/ pine) painted (falu red).
- Foundation: edge-insulated slab on ground (platta på mark) on crushed stone + EPS (frost-protected shallow foundation); crawlspace/ basement with ICF or block in some cases.
- Roof: prefab trusses, ventilated cold roof, felt/ sheet metal/ tile; snow loads drive heavy members; roof overhang big.
- Multi-storey: CLT (Sweden/ Norway pioneers e.g., Stockholm Wood City), glulam frames, volumetric modules, Mjøstårnet 18 storeys (Norway).
- Denmark: brick-clad masonry cavity wall + light-concrete block; also timber.
- Heating: district heating/ heat pumps; triple glazing; mechanical ventilation with heat recovery (MVHR) standard in new builds.
Signature details: vapour barrier placement and taping, wall wind barrier; foundation with perimeter EPS skirt; chimney/ flue in cold climate.

### 2.5 France / Spain / Italy (and Portugal, Greece)
- France: concrete block (parpaing) or monomur brick (brique alvéolaire 30-37 cm) with thin-bed mortar; ITI (interior insulation + plasterboard) or ITE; RC ring beam (chaînage horizontal) and vertical ties (chaînage vertical) around openings; floors: poutrelles-hourdis (precast joists + hollow EPS/ concrete blocks + 4-5 cm topping); roof: timber truss (fermettes) + tuiles canal / mécaniques; slab-on-grade (dallage) on terre-plein or vide sanitaire.
- Spain: RC frame (pilares + forjados unidireccionales with bovedillas) + double-leaf fired-clay brick infill: outer 1/2 pie (11.5 cm) ladrillo cara vista/ rendered + cavity with insulation + inner hollow brick (tabique 7 cm); forjado reticular (waffle slab) common in apartment blocks; roof flat (cubierta plana invertida with gravel/ paving) or tile; strip/ pad footings with tie beams (vigas de atado/ centradoras) ⚠ verify nomenclature.
- Italy: RC frame + hollow clay block infill (laterizio forato/ blocchi alveolati) 30-38 cm monolithic or with "cappotto" ETICS; latero-cemento floor slab (clay-block ribbed slab); load-bearing masonry in historic centres; seismic ties (cordoli) at every floor; timber roofs (tetto in legno) with tiles (coppi).
- Greece: RC frame + double-brick infill, pilotis, flat roofs with insulation; heavy seismic detailing.
Signature details: ring beam/ cordolo at every slab edge, brick infill spacing from columns (movement joint), forjado in section with bovedillas, ceramic roof tile detail, shutter box (persiana) integrated.

### 2.6 Gulf (UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain)
Residential villa (typical 2-3 storeys + roof)
- Structure: RC isolated or raft foundations → RC columns (250x250, 300x600 typical; ⚠ verify) → beams + solid or ribbed slabs (200-250 mm; Gulf slabs often 200 mm solid with drop beams, or flat slab for larger spans) → blockwork infill.
- Foundations: isolated pad footings + tie beams/ground beams over 2-3 storey villas with sound ground; raft (mat) where groundwater high or soil weak; piles for towers or collapsible/ sabkha (salt flats) soils. Sabkha: high water table, chloride + sulphate attack → sulphate-resisting cement or blended (GGBS/ silica fume) concrete, bitumen/ epoxy coating on buried concrete, thick cover (50-75 mm typical in aggressive soils; ⚠ verify), PE membrane, possibly dewatering or soil replacement; settlement from salt dissolution. Very site-specific — follow geotechnical report.
- Blinding 50-75 mm lean concrete, bitumen coat/ polythene to underside of footings in aggressive ground.
- Walls: 200 mm hollow concrete blocks (solid blocks below DPC/ ground), 150 mm or 100 mm internal partitions; thermal blocks (insulated hollow blocks, 200 mm with EPS core or ceramic/ lightweight aggregate); cavity walls (outer 100 mm + 50 mm insulation + inner 100 mm) in newer energy-code villas; external render 20 mm cement-sand in two coats + paint or stone/ GRC/ GRP cladding; internal 15 mm gypsum or cement plaster + paint.
- Slabs: ribbed (hourdi: hollow concrete blocks between RC ribs) or hollow-core precast + 50-75 mm topping, or solid. Roof: slab → screed-to-falls (lightweight foam concrete 50-150 mm, min 1:100 fall) → waterproofing (APP/ SBS bituminous 2-ply 3+4 mm, or PU/ cementitious in some) → protection board → thermal insulation XPS 50-75 mm (inverted roof) or under membrane (warm roof) → separation layer (geotextile) → gravel ballast or concrete tiles or screed + tile finish. Roof tanks (see §5), satellite, AC outdoor units on roof or on slab.
- Wet areas: 2-coat cementitious waterproofing (or liquid polyurethane) turned up 150-300 mm walls, 1800 mm in shower, membrane under screed, floor drain in 1:100 fall, ceramic/ porcelain tile.
- Finishes: terrazzo/ marble (floors); gypsum false ceiling with cove light + concealed AC duct; GRC/ GRP/ stone cladding to façade, grill/ mashrabiya screens; large boundary wall (2.4-3 m) and gates, majlis (separate reception), maid's room, driver room, annex, basement parking in some.
Precast villas
- Mass-housing programmes in Qatar/ Saudi/ UAE use precast concrete wall panels or load-bearing precast cassettes, hollow-core floors; joints grouted; services via embedded conduits and chases. ⚠ verify panel thickness (commonly 150-200 mm with insulation sandwich panels).
Commercial / towers
- RC flat slab or post-tensioned flat slab 220-300 mm; core wall with shear walls; raft + piles; curtain wall unitised glazing with spandrel insulation, solar-control glass, solar shading (fins); steel structure for stadiums/ airports; precast cladding panels; chilled-water district cooling (UAE, Qatar) with plant rooms, FCU/ AHU risers.
- Dubai/ Abu Dhabi code requires specific thermal U-values per wall and roof → thermal block, insulated cavity, double glazing. Insulation thickness is determined by energy codes (see country files).
Signature details
- Parapet 1.0 m min over roof (RoT, safety), upstand 150-300 mm of membrane up parapet with chase, roof drain/ gargoyle (overflow), thermal break at slab edge, expansion joint in long parapet/ screed, sloped ground floor external areas.
- Large 3.0-3.6 m floor-to-floor typical for villas; 2.4-2.7 m clear ceiling; double-height lobbies.

### 2.7 Egypt
Residential
- RC skeleton ("هيكل خرساني"): isolated footings tied by strap beams (shared ground beams) or raft in poor ground; columns 25x60 to 25x100 cm (RoT), beams cast monolithically with slab; slabs commonly solid with drop beams or hollow-block ribbed ("بلاطة هوردي") with embedded beams — common floor system; flat slabs for larger spans / fast construction. ⚠ verify exact distribution.
- Infill: red clay brick 12 cm (half-brick) for partitions and 25 cm (full brick) for external walls — or cement/ hollow concrete block 20 cm, sometimes AAC/ clay-hollow blocks (12 or 25 cm), local "Tob Ahmar". External wall 25 cm single leaf with render, or cavity (12 + air + 12) in better buildings; plaster 15-20 mm (three-coat: "bayaan", "mahara", "tashteeb" / bayaan → mahara → "tashteeb"), painted or cladded.
- Incremental vertical construction: columns left with exposed starter bars at roof for later floors; roof stub columns visible; foundations designed (sometimes) for future floors — typical in informal/ semi-formal housing; draw "starter bars" and "future floors" as dashed, show roof slab as non-final.
- Roof: waterproofing (bituminous membranes), foam concrete for slope, thermal insulation (XPS/ EPS) increasingly, tiles/ screed; water tanks (fiberglass/ PE) on roof; satellite dishes, solar water heaters.
- Finishes: ceramic/ porcelain floor tiles, marble thresholds, aluminium/ uPVC/ wooden windows with exterior shutters (roll shutters "persienne"); gypsum-board false ceilings in better finishing.
Commercial / towers
- RC frame/ flat slab; post-tensioned slabs rare; piles for towers (New Capital, Cairo), curtain walls, precast in factories; heavy use of brick infill even in towers (shear walls + brick).
Signature details: strap beam in foundation plan, "column box"/ "tie beam" layout, brick coursing (stretcher vs header), lintel beams over all openings, stone cladding (limestone/ marble) on façades with metal anchors.

### 2.8 Levant (Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, Palestine, Israel) and Turkey
- Jordan/ Amman: stone-clad RC frame — RC columns and slab, block infill (20 cm hollow concrete block), 8-10 cm cut limestone cladding (local "Hajar Ammani") fixed with metal ties and mortar; thermal insulation layer + stone; ribbed slab with hollow block ("hordi") and 25-30 cm thickness; flat roof with gravel/ tile; basement garage with retaining wall + waterproofing. Stone patterns: tubzeh, musamman, kasbad (cut, dressed faces). ⚠ verify names/ thickness.
- Lebanon: RC frame + double-leaf hollow-block with 5 cm cavity insulation or single 20 cm; stone or render finish; sloped red-tile roof in mountain areas.
- Israel: RC frame + AAC or hollow block, "mamad" (safe room reinforced RC 20 cm min ⚠ verify) in new dwellings; large use of aluminium roller shutters; plastered and painted or stone-clad.
- Turkey: RC frame (Betonarme karkas) with "tuğla" hollow clay brick infill 13.5 cm internal, 19 cm external + mantolama (EPS/ rock wool ETICS 5 cm+ with mesh and render); ribbed slabs with hollow clay bricks ("asmolen") or flat slabs; shear walls (perde) in mid-rise; hip-tiled roof (çatı) with timber structure over top slab in many apartment buildings; elevator core RC.
Signature details: stone-cladding anchors, mantolama (insulation) on drawings, shear wall layouts (perde), basement waterproofing, roof timber attic.

### 2.9 India / Pakistan / Bangladesh / Sri Lanka
- RCC frame: columns 230x450 to 300x600 mm, beams 230x450, slab 125-150 mm; brick infill 230 mm external / 115 mm internal (burnt clay, fly-ash brick, or AAC block 200 mm in newer builds); 12-20 mm cement plaster; lintels + chajja (sun shade) projections 600-900 mm; sill band; plinth beam; terrace roof with brickbat coba (waterproofing with lime/ cement over slab) or APP membrane + China mosaic/ tiles; overhead water tank on terrace + sump underground + pump; staircase RC; mumty (stair head).
- Foundation: isolated pad/ combined footing/ raft; piles in soft soils; plinth beam at ground level; filling to plinth level (300-600 mm) with compacted earth, DPC layer 40 mm PCC + waterproofing compound.
- Load-bearing brick (1-1.5 brick, 230-345 mm) for 1-3 storey rural/ low-cost; stone masonry in hill regions (Pakistan north); bamboo/ wattle in Bangladesh villages; precast in cities increasingly.
- Commercial: RCC frame with flat slab/ waffle slab, steel PEB (pre-engineered buildings) for sheds, curtain walls on towers, brick infill dominates.
Signature details: sunshade, plinth beam, damp-proof course, "lintel + chajja" combos, terrace slope (1:100 to 1:60 ⚠ verify), parapet 1.0-1.2 m, brick bond (English/ Flemish), brickbat coba detail.

### 2.10 China
- Residential towers: RC shear-wall (剪力墙) structure with RC slab 100-120 mm, 20+ storeys; walls 160-300 mm; infill partitions AAC or lightweight block 100-200 mm; external insulation (EPS/ XPS/ rock wool) with thin render or stone/ tile cladding; windows aluminium/ PVC with thermal break.
- Low-rise: brick-concrete (砖混) structure, often with ring beam (圈梁) and constructional column (构造柱 GZ) — comparable to confined masonry; common for older rural/ small-town housing.
- Commercial: RC frame + core tube, steel mega-frame, steel-concrete composite; unitised curtain wall; fast precast cladding; raised/ thick fit-out MEP riser (管井).
- Prefab: PC (prefabricated concrete) components with grouted sleeves; steel modular; bamboo/ wood rare (some Rammed earth tulou heritage).
Signature details: 构造柱/ 圈梁 callouts, 防水 layering on roof (waterproof grade), balcony floor-to-ceiling window with 防护栏, 保温层 thickness in external wall legend.

### 2.11 Japan
- Timber post-and-beam (在来工法/ kenchiku) still the majority for detached houses: 105 or 120 mm square posts @ 910 mm module (RoT: 3 shaku grid; 1P = 910x910 mm), plates, beams, braces (suji-kai) + structural plywood; foundation continuous RC strip footing/ mat (ベタ基礎, slab-on-grade with ground-bearing mat) 150 mm thick with anchor bolts; underfloor crawlspace ventilation; roofing: ceramic tile (kawara) or metal; walls: mortar-lath or siding (kensetsu-sa), plasterboard interior; sliding fusuma, tatami rooms with raised floor.
- 2x4 (platform) framing: grown from 1970s; ~10-15 % of starts ⚠ verify; 2x4 @ 455 mm o.c. (≈ 18"), structural panels.
- Steel-framed prefab (Sekisui Heim, Daiwa House, Toyota Home): light-gauge steel frames, factory panels; ALC panels (autoclaved lightweight concrete) as exterior cladding on steel frames.
- Mid/ high-rise: RC, SRC (steel-reinforced concrete) and steel; base isolation (laminated rubber bearings/ sliding bearings) and dampers common in hospitals, offices, residential towers; base isolation also used in some houses (isolation under wooden foundations) ⚠ verify; brace-dampers (oil/ viscous).
- Wall build: ~120-200 mm total in timber houses; double-glazed aluminium/ resin frames; thermal performance rising.
Signature details: 910 mm grid on plan, shear-wall layout (耐力壁), joint hardware (hold-down plates, T-/L- plates), foundation cut with underfloor vents (床下換気口), seismic gap between buildings, fire-resistance rated joints.

### 2.12 South-East Asia
- RC frame + clay brick infill (Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia, Philippines, Malaysia): columns 200x200 to 300x300 for 1-3 storeys; brick or concrete hollow block 100-200 mm; 15 mm plaster; flat/ shallow-pitched slab or pitched metal/ tile roof with timber/ steel purlins over RC slab; louvered shutters, perforated blocks for ventilation, deep overhangs (1.0-1.5 m).
- Philippines: CHB (concrete hollow blocks) 100/150 mm, RC columns every 3 m for 1-2 storeys; steel roof trusses; typhoon/ seismic hold-downs.
- Stilt houses (traditional): timber or bamboo piles, floor raised 1.0-3.0 m above ground for flooding/ ventilation; modern stilt-lite RC piloti ground-floor parking.
- Singapore/ HK: PPVC (prefabricated prefinished volumetric construction), precast bathroom pods, steel/ RC composite towers; strict QA; high rise with RC walls.
- Vietnam "tube houses": narrow (3-5 m wide, 15-20 m deep) RC frame row houses with brick; internal light wells; stairs at side.
Signature details: sun/ rain shading overhangs, termite barrier and treated timber, flood levels, raft foundation, piles in Mekong/ Bangkok soft clay.

### 2.13 Australia / New Zealand
- Australia: brick veneer on timber (or light-gauge steel) frame — 110 mm brick outer skin, 40 mm cavity, foil sarking/ wall wrap, 90 mm timber studs (90x35 or 90x45 mm) @ 450/600 mm o.c., insulation R2.0-2.7 batts, 10 mm plasterboard; or lightweight clad (fibre-cement, weatherboard); double-brick in older; AAC/ concrete panel rising; slab-on-ground: waffle-raft slabs (EPS pods 175-375 mm deep on 1200 mm grid, ~85 mm top slab, 300 mm edge beam) to AS 2870 site classes; stiffened raft; pier-and-beam in reactive/ sloping sites; roof trusses @ 900 mm, Colorbond steel sheet or concrete/ terracotta tiles on battens, sarking; eaves 450-600 mm; bushfire zones (BAL) alter materials.
- NZ: timber frame dominant (90 mm studs @ 600 mm with bracing elements e.g., plywood bracing panels), weatherboard/ fibre-cement/ brick veneer; slab on ground with edge beam; piles for timber floors; seismic bracing lines, hold-down bolts and straps.
- Commercial: tilt-up panels, steel portal frames (sheds), RC frame; steel stud + FC sheet in mid-rise.
Signature details: waffle pod slab section, sarking foil, weep holes in brick veneer, control joints every ~6 m in brickwork, termite management (physical barrier/ chemical) shown on slab plan, bracing walls (shear) plan.

### 2.14 Latin America
- Confined masonry: Chile, Colombia, Peru, Mexico, Argentina — hollow clay brick (ladrillo) 12-14 cm walls, tie-columns at corners/ junctions/ openings and every ≤4 m, tie-beams at every floor; RC slab or precast joist-and-block (vigueta y bovedilla; viguetas pretensadas) ; roof slab with chimney/ tank (tinaco in Mexico, tanque elevado); wall plaster (repello) 15-20 mm.
- RC frame + brick/ block infill for mid-rise; Brazil: RC frame + ceramic hollow brick (bloco cerâmico) with RC pillar-beam "estrutura convencional", flat slabs (laje lisa) and "laje nervurada" with EPS fillers; Mexico: reinforced block "casa de interés social" with vigas/ castillos.
- Informal housing (favelas, colonias): self-built incremental RC frame + brick, rebar starter columns at roof for future floors (analogous to Egypt).
- Adobe/ bahareque in Andes, Central America, with seismic strengthening needs.
- Steel: Mexican light steel (Panel W); commercial: steel + concrete deck, tensioned slab.
Signature details: castillos (tie columns) and cadenas/ dalas (tie beams) with stirrups, anchor bars at slab, "pretil" parapet, vigueta-bovedilla slab detail, "firme" slab on ground.

### 2.15 Sub-Saharan Africa and N. Africa
- Concrete block (sandcrete/ hollow cement block 150/ 225 mm) infill with RC columns/ ring beams — dominant across West/ East/ Southern Africa; strip/ pad footing; corrugated iron/ aluminium sheet roof on timber trusses; plaster; verandas.
- South Africa: face brick cavity walls (110 + 50 cavity + 110 mm), light steel roof trusses (nail-plated), IBR sheeting or concrete tiles; slab-on-ground with DPM; "RDP" housing small single-storey.
- Adobe/ rammed earth (Sahel, Ethiopia, Morocco): thick earth walls, flat earthen roofs or thatch; stabilised compressed earth blocks (CEB) increasingly; lime or cement plaster, plinth in stone.
- N. Africa (Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, Libya): RC frame + hollow red-brick infill (brique creuse 8-12 trous), French influence (poutrelles hourdis floors); courtyard houses; thick walls, flat roofs.
Signature details: block coursing, ring beam at eaves, roof timber truss connections, rainwater harvesting tanks, surface drainage (stormwater) and soakaway, termite treatment.

### 3.5 Ground floor slab (with insulation, cold/ moderate climates)

### 5.1 Embedded vs cavity
| System | Route | Notes for drawings |

- Europe: central heating (boiler or heat pump) with radiators/ underfloor; heat-recovery ventilation; no AC in many homes (UK, DE, Nordics) though split units increase.
- Japan: bath unit (unit bath prefab), individual water heaters (ecocute), mini-split A/C.

## 7. Common mistakes when an AI drafts details for the wrong region

| Mistake | Wrong region assumption | Correct local practice |
|---|---|---|
| Drawing timber studs + OSB for a Gulf, Egyptian or Indian villa | US platform framing | RC frame + block infill; plaster; no stud walls except partitions in drywall |
| 2x4 @ 16" o.c. in UK/ EU/ Nordic details | Imperial spacing | Metric 400/ 600 mm o.c. (UK 600 trussed rafters; Nordic 600; Germany 625) |
| Showing cavity wall with brick outer leaf in Gulf | UK | Gulf: render on block, single leaf or insulated cavity block; brick not typical |
| Forgetting DPC/ moisture in UK | - | DPC 150 mm above ground, cavity trays |
| Using frost depth footings in Gulf/ Egypt/ S. India | North America | No frost; depth set by bearing stratum, groundwater, sabkha; not frost |
| No sunk slab / slope in wet areas | - | Gulf/ Egypt/ India need sunk slab and falls to drain |
| Omitting roof tanks, pumps, AC condenser space | US | Required in Gulf/ Egypt/ India/ Levant; show on roof plan |
| Gulf: omitting thermal insulation + waterproofing in roof | - | Required by code; layers and thickness per local energy rules |
| Egypt: using 200 mm hollow block when red brick 25 cm is standard | Gulf | Specify red brick or hollow clay as local standard; cement block possible |
| Germany: wall labelled "300 mm brick" without ETICS | - | Total wall thickness includes insulation 140-240 mm and render |
| US: showing brick bearing walls in 2-storey house | UK/ Gulf | Brick is veneer only (non-structural, tied to frame) |
| UK: electric sockets drawn as NEMA | US | BS 1363 13 A; Gulf same |
| Japan: using 16" or 600 mm grids | - | 910 mm / 455 mm modules; tatami |
| Confined masonry shown without tie columns/ beams | - | Required: RC confinement at corners/ openings/ each floor |
| Latin America: "stud wall" for exterior | US | Brick/ block + tie columns |
| Flat roof with no fall/ drain | - | Fall 1:80-1:100 min; drains and overflows |
| Using imperial units outside US/ Canada/ Liberia/ Myanmar | - | Metric |
| Basement shown in Gulf/ Egypt without tanking/ dewatering | - | High water table: tanking, waterstop, sump pumps |
| Mixing steel stud walls (CFS) in places with no CFS supply chain | - | Check local availability; block still dominates |
| Wrong ceiling heights (e.g. 8' ceilings in Gulf) | US | Gulf 2.7-3.2 m clear; Europe 2.4-2.6 m |
| Cavity insulation detailing for climates without need | Cold | Hot-arid: external insulation or cavity; shade rather than R-value |
| Door/ window opening sizes & swing conventions | US | Door leaf 900-1000 mm (US 36"), Gulf 900-1000 mm, Europe 900 mm; verify; main door wider in Gulf (1100-1200) |
| Using drywall ceilings with insulation in Gulf where gypsum false ceilings hide AC | - | Gypsum false ceilings with AC plenum common |
| Not drawing starter bars / future floors in incremental markets | - | Egypt, Latin America, India: draw/ note |
| Not showing stone cladding anchors in Jordan | - | Required |
| Regionally missing items: mamad (Israel), majlis (Gulf), pooja room (India), genkan (Japan), tatami layout | - | Include local program rooms |
Process fixes for AI: (1) identify region and climate before choosing system; (2) look up country file for code values; (3) state assumptions and mark "⚠ verify" when uncertain; (4) check units, module and grid; (5) show local trades conventions (plaster, tile, waterproofing); (6) never silently default to US framing.

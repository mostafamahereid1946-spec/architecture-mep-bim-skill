# Civil / Site - Method Reference

Tags: **[RoT]** = rule of thumb (typical range; confirm with local authority/code); **[CODE]** = take from country file; **⚠ verify** = unconfirmed. Metric first, imperial in brackets.

## 1. Workflow

| Step | Output |
|---|---|
| 1. Inputs | Survey (boundary, topo, existing services), title deed/plot plan, setbacks/FAR/coverage [CODE], geotech report, utility availability letters/NOCs, arch ground-floor plan |

| 3. Site layout | Building footprint within setbacks, entrances, driveway, parking, fire access, services corridors |

| 5. Drainage | Surface water paths, storm network, foul network, outfalls |
| 6. Utilities | Water, sewer, storm, power, telecom, gas, district cooling connections and meters |
| 7. Pavement/landscape | Road/parking geometry, pavement build-up, kerbs, walls, gates |

| 9. Drawings + schedules | Section 9 |
| 10. Check | Section 10 |

## 2. Site plan content

Mandatory elements on site plan (scale 1:200-1:500; 1:1000 for large sites):
- Plot boundary with bearings/distances and plot number; adjacent plots/roads with names and widths; north arrow; scale bar; key plan/location plan.
- Setback lines with dimensions (front/side/rear) [CODE]; building footprint (ground floor outline, overhangs dashed), roof overhang/balcony projections.
- Levels: FFL of each building, road centreline levels, boundary corner levels, spot levels on drive, parking, paths.
- Contours existing (dashed/grey) and proposed (solid); slope arrows with %.
- Access: gates (width, swing), driveway with slopes, parking bays (numbered), pedestrian paths, accessible route, ramps, bollards.
- Services: water meter/tank, sewer connection & manholes, storm drains, septic/soakaway, electrical meter room/RMU/substation, telecom manhole, gas; street lighting poles.
- Fire access: fire truck route/turning, hydrants, FDC, assembly point.
- Boundary wall/fence (height, type), retaining walls, trees to keep/remove, landscaping zones, outdoor equipment (generators, chiller yards).
- Area statement table: plot area, built-up area (BUA) per floor, coverage %, FAR, parking required vs provided, green area %.
- Legend, abbreviations, notes (datum, units, coordinate system).

## 3. Levels, datum and contours

- Datum: national vertical datum (e.g., mean sea level based); local datum permitted if tied to BM. State: "Levels are in metres relative to <datum>. Benchmark BM1 = xx.xxx at <location>."
- Notation: RL/EL/Level (reduced level); FFL = finished floor level; FGL = finished ground level; EGL = existing ground level; IL = invert level; CL = cover level (manhole top); TOW/BOW = top/bottom of wall; TOK/FL = top of kerb / flow line.
- FFL (plinth) above finished ground: **[RoT]** 150-600 mm; typical villa ground floor 450-600 above adjacent finished ground / ≥ 300-450 above road crown (Gulf), ≥ 150-300 (Egypt, UK ~150 min above ground ⚠ verify) so water drains away; flood risk areas: higher [CODE].
- Contours: contour interval 0.25-0.5 m for plots, 1 m for large sites; index contour every 5th labelled; closer lines = steeper; contour crossing a road bends upstream (V points to higher ground); contours cannot cross each other except on cliff/walls.
- Interpolation: level at point = L1 + (L2 - L1) x (d1 / D) between two known spot levels.
- Cut/fill: volumes via grid/prismoidal or average end area (V = (A1 + A2)/2 x L); aim to balance (cut ≈ fill x compaction factor); topsoil strip 150-300 mm stockpiled; swell 10-30% on excavation, shrink 5-15% on compaction [RoT] ⚠ verify per soil; compaction ≥ 95% modified Proctor under slabs/pavement (98% for fills > 1.5 m ⚠) [CODE/geotech].
- Gradients notation: slope as ratio (1:20), percent (5%), or angle; always state which; arrow points down-slope.

## 4. Grading and surface drainage slopes [RoT unless noted]

| Surface | Min | Typical | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paved area (concrete/asphalt) | 0.5-1% | 1-2% | ~5% (accessible walkway ≤ 5% without ramp rules) |
| Ground sloping away from building | 2% for first 3 m (ADA/IRC: 5% for 3 m (10 ft) on soil) ⚠ | 2-5% | - |
| Lawn / landscape | 1% | 2-5% | 1:3 (33%) mowable; > 1:3 stabilise/terrace |
| Swale/ditch (grass) | 1% | 1-5% | 5-8% ⚠ |
| Hard-paved drain channel | 0.5% | 1% | - |
| Car park surface | 1% (flat), 1.5-2% preferred | 1-2% | 5% (accessible stalls ≤ 2% each way) |
| Road crossfall (camber) | 2% | 2-3% | 4% ⚠ |
| Road longitudinal gradient | 0.5% (kerb flow) | 1-6% | 8-10% local roads; 12-15% driveway |
| Driveway to garage | 1% | 2-8% | 12-15% with transitions (1:2 ramps ends) ⚠ |
| Pedestrian walk crossfall | 1% | 1-2% | 2% (accessible) |
| Roof/terrace slabs | 1:100 (1%) | 1:80-1:60 | - |
| Pitched embankment (cut/fill) | - | 1:2 - 1:3 | 1:1.5 with protection ⚠ |
| Accessible ramp | - | 1:20 | 1:12 [CODE] |

- Retaining wall needed where level difference > ~0.6-1.0 m between cut and fill and slope exceeds stable slope; retaining wall detail with drain (weep holes + gravel backfill + geotextile), waterproofing, footing; fence/guard at top > 0.6 m drop [CODE].
- Drain surface water away from boundary walls to avoid flooding neighbours; sheet flow not to cross onto neighbour [CODE].
- Landscaping areas lower than pavement by 25-50 to avoid mulch spill; area drains in each low point.

### 5.2 Parking geometry (car 4.8 x 1.85 m typical design vehicle; stall figures [RoT])
| Angle | Stall width | Stall length | Aisle width (2-way / 1-way) | Module (stall+aisle+stall) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 90° | 2.4-2.7 (2.5 std, 2.7 comfort) | 4.8-5.5 (5.0 std) | 6.0-7.0 | ~16-17 m |
| 60° | 2.5 | 5.2 | 4.2-5.5 one-way | ~16 |
| 45° | 2.5 | 5.0 | 3.5-4.5 one-way | ~14.5 |
| Parallel | 2.0-2.5 | 6.0-6.7 | 3.0-3.5 | - |
- Columns in parking: 7.5-8.4 m bay accommodates 3 x 2.5 with columns ≤ 0.6 m outside stalls; minimum clear width stall 2.3; column projection ≤ 0.15-0.30 into stall ⚠.
- Accessible stalls: width 3.6-3.9 m (incl. 1.2-1.5 access aisle, shared allowed) [CODE]; counts [CODE] (typ. 2-5% of total, min 1); located nearest entrance on level route; signs.
- Parking count [CODE]: e.g., 1 per 40-100 m² of office/retail; 1-2 per dwelling; check local.
- Ramps (car park): max 1:8 - 1:6 for straight ramps (12.5-16%) [RoT] ⚠ [CODE]; transitions 3.0-3.6 m long at half slope (≈ 1:12-1:16) to avoid scraping; ramp width 3.0-3.5 per lane (straight), 3.65-4.0 curved lane; inner radius ≥ 5-6 m, outer 9-12; headroom ≥ 2.1-2.4 m (2.1 typical 2.0 min ⚠), ≥ 2.6 at accessible/van routes; ramp slabs with grooves for grip.
- Turning: car min outer turning radius 5.5-7.5 m (inner 3.5-4.5); aisle 6-7 m with 90° stalls lets 90° turn into stall; dead-end aisles extend 1 m (turn-around); service truck 8-10 m outer 12 ⚠.
- Loading bay: 3.5 x 7-12 m with 4.2-4.5 m clear height; apron for turning.
- Bicycle: 0.6 x 1.8-2.0 per bike; motorbike 1.0 x 2.0; EV charging space 2.5-3.0 x 5.5, 1 per N stalls [CODE].
- Landscape islands every 10-15 stalls; wheel stops 100 high 0.9 m from stall end ⚠; 1.5% minimum paved slope to drains.

## 6. Site drainage and sewerage (design method)

### 6.1 Surface water (storm)
1. Catchment areas by surface type; runoff coefficient C [RoT]: roofs/asphalt/concrete 0.90-0.95; paving blocks 0.7-0.8; gravel 0.4-0.6; lawn 0.10-0.30; landscape 0.15-0.30.
2. Rainfall intensity i (mm/h) for design return period (2-10 yr private, 10-25 yr large/critical) and duration = time of concentration (5-10 min start) from IDF curve of local authority [CODE] (arid zones: 50-150 mm/h ⚠).
3. Rational method: Q (L/s) = C x i x A / 3.6 with A in ha, i in mm/h (Q in L/s = 2.78 x C x i x A); or Q (m³/s) = C i A / 360 (A ha). For A in m²: Q (L/s) = C x i x A / 3600.
4. Pipe size: Manning V = (1/n) R^(2/3) S^(1/2); Q = A V; n = 0.013 concrete, 0.010-0.011 uPVC/HDPE, 0.012-0.014 for GRP/old ⚠; full-bore capacity at gradient S; design at 70-80% depth for large pipes, full for small ⚠.
5. Limits [RoT]: min velocity 0.75-1.0 m/s (self-cleansing); max 3-4.5 m/s; min pipe 150-225 mm private site (300 mm public/road crossing ⚠); min cover 0.6 m (landscape), 0.9-1.0 m (traffic), 1.2+ (heavy ⚠) or concrete surround.
6. Inlets: gully/area drains/channel drains at low points (gully spacing 20-30 m paved ⚠ by catchment); catch basins with sump; trench drains at gates/ramps (channel 100-300 wide).
7. Manholes at all changes of direction, gradient, size, junctions, and spacing ≤ 50-100 m storm / ≤ 30-45 m foul-private ⚠; MH size: 1.0-1.2 m dia (depth to 3 m), 1.5 for deeper; cover loading class D400 (traffic) / B125 (footways) / C250 ⚠ (EN 124).
8. Pipe gradient min [RoT]: foul 100 mm 1:80 (UK AD H ⚠) / typical 1:40-1:60; 150 mm 1:150 / typical 1:60-1:100; storm gradient follows ground ≥ 1:200 ⚠. Do not decrease pipe size downstream; maintain crown-matching at junctions (or drop MH).
9. Outfall: public storm drain, soakaway, detention/retention tank, SuDS (swales, permeable paving, infiltration trench); detention volume = max(inflow - allowable discharge) over time; infiltration needs percolation test; soakaway ≥ 5 m from building / ≥ 1.5-3 m from boundary ⚠ [CODE].
10. Pumped storm for basements/ramps (sump + duplex pumps).

### 6.2 Foul sewer and septic
- Gravity from building to public MH via inspection chamber (IC) at property boundary/ 1 m inside plot; connect at invert ≥ crown or into side of public MH with drop; avoid backflow with levels (building drain lower than public sewer -> pump/backwater valve).
- Septic: tank + soakaway/leach field or holding tank; sizes per daily flow: septic volume ≥ 2-3 x daily flow / 24 h retention ⚠; location accessible to tanker, ≥ 5-6 m from building, ≥ 1.5-3 m from boundary, ≥ 15-30 m from wells ⚠ [CODE]; ventilation stack; baffles; grease trap for kitchens (retention 3-5 min flow ⚠).
- Flow estimate: 80% of water demand; peak factor 2.0-4.0 ⚠.
- Separate foul and storm; combine only where authority requires.

### 6.3 Invert level schedule method
IL_downstream = IL_upstream - (S x L). Depth to invert = CL - IL. Check crown matching: crown_down ≤ crown_up. Track minimum cover. Schedule columns: Pipe ID | From MH | To MH | Length m | Diameter | Gradient 1:x | Flow L/s | Velocity m/s | IL up | IL down | Cover level up/down | Depth up/down | Remarks.
Manhole schedule: MH ID | Type | Cover level | Invert levels (each pipe) | Depth | Size | Cover class | Chainage/coords.

## 7. Utilities connections

| Utility | Method | Rules [RoT] |
|---|---|---|
| Water supply | Utility main -> boundary meter chamber (isolation valve, meter, backflow preventer) -> trench to ground tank (air gap fill) | Pipe depth cover 0.6-1.0 m (traffic ≥ 0.9), size DN25-DN50 villa / DN50-DN100 buildings by demand; warning tape; thrust blocks at bends; testing 1.5x working pressure ⚠ |
| Sewer | Gravity to public MH (see 6.2) | Separation from water mains: 3 m horizontal (US 3 m / 10 ft), sewer below water at crossings with 0.3-0.5 m vertical clearance ⚠ [CODE] |
| Storm | To public storm or on-site disposal | Check authority approval |
| Electricity | MV cable to RMU/substation/kiosk, LV to meter room/main panel | Substation location accessible from public road for 24 h utility access, 1.0-2.0 clearance each side, 3-6 m from building/openings (fire) ⚠ [CODE]; ducts 100-150 mm uPVC encased in concrete with draw pits every 30-50 m, depth 0.6-1.0 (0.8 typical LV, 1.0 MV); warning tape + marker |
| Telecom / fibre | Utility duct to boundary chamber, 2-4 x 100 mm ducts | Separate from power ≥ 0.3-0.5 m ⚠ |
| Gas | Utility main -> regulator -> meter | Ventilated gas meter cabinet at boundary, ≥ 1 m from openings ⚠ [CODE] |
| District cooling | ETS room with plate HX, supply/return DN100-DN400 | Space for ETS 20-60 m² per 1000 TR ⚠ per utility |
| Street lighting | Poles 6-10 m, spacing 25-40 m ⚠, pits | Lux per road class [CODE] |
| Fire water | Hydrant main loop DN150 min ⚠ | Hydrant spacing ≤ 90-150 m, within 100-150 m of entrances ⚠ [CODE] |
Typical trench separation [RoT]: services in common trench corridor with min 0.3 m between different services, power above telecom, water above sewer; building services corridor 2-3 m wide along boundary; do not run under building footprint.
Underground service drawings: layout with utility legend (colour-coded lines), crossings with clearance dimension, depth/profile, thrust blocks, chambers (sizes), trench details, pipe bedding (150 sand surround / granular), marker tapes and tracer wire.

## 8. Setting-out and survey grid systems

### 8.1 Coordinate frames
- **National/projected grid**: Easting (E/X) and Northing (N/Y) in metres, e.g., UTM zones (UAE ~ 39/40 N; Saudi 37-39 N; Egypt 35-36 N), national grids (Dubai Local Transverse Mercator DLTM, Qatar National Grid (QND95), Egypt Transverse Mercator (ETM) belts, UK OSGB36 National Grid, US State Plane (US survey feet) ⚠ verify per project/authority). Confirm datum (WGS84/ITRF, local), projection, units (m vs ft; US survey foot vs international foot differ by 2 ppm), and vertical datum.
- **Local site grid / building grid**: orthogonal axes aligned to building; rotated vs national grid by angle θ; transformation: E = E0 + x cosθ - y sinθ; N = N0 + x sinθ + y cosθ where (E0, N0) = origin of local grid in national grid. Record θ, origin and scale factor on drawing.
- Grid vs ground distance: ground = grid / combined scale factor (CSF = k x height factor); ignore on small sites unless required (CSF ~ 0.9996-1.0003 ⚠). True north ≠ grid north (convergence); mark both if needed.
- Typical gridlines: letters (A, B, C) one direction, numbers (1, 2, 3) other; same grid in arch/struct/MEP; setting-out to gridlines with offsets.

### 8.2 Setting-out data to provide
- Control points/stations (Name, E, N, Level) at least 2-3 intervisible stations + benchmark outside work area (protected by concrete + marking).
- Building corner coordinates table: Pt | E | N | Description (e.g., "Grid A/1 outside face of wall"). Also gridline intersection coordinates, offsets (distance from boundary), column centres, ramp start/end, gates, manhole centres, pole positions.
- Angle/bearing + distance (polar) or intersection data; bearings in degrees-minutes-seconds (WCB 0-360°) or decimals; distances horizontal (not slope).
- Levels: FFL, formation levels, slab tops, invert levels as RL; transfer by level line from BM; tolerances [RoT]: setting-out ±5-10 mm for buildings, ±25 for earthworks/roads ⚠ [CODE/spec].
- Instruments: total station (angle+distance), GNSS RTK (cm accuracy, needs open sky), digital level, laser; checks via diagonals (diagonal lengths equal for rectangles - 3-4-5 / Pythagoras), redundant measurements.
- Boundary pillar/peg verification before starting; as-built survey after structure; final survey to authority for completion certificate [CODE].
- File formats: CSV (P,E,N,Z,Code), DXF/DWG with layers, LandXML.

### 8.3 Survey drawing content (topographic survey)
Boundary points, spot levels on grid 5-10 m (3-5 near features), contours, existing buildings, kerbs, roads with levels, poles, trees (trunk dia, type), manholes (cover/invert), utilities (visible/located), walls/fences, ditches, water bodies, benchmarks, coordinate ticks, north arrow, datum statement, surveyor certificate, date, accuracy statement.

## 10. Coordination with other disciplines
- **Architecture**: FFL vs road/ground level; driveway slope into garage; threshold; setbacks; boundary wall; ramps/steps; accessible route; waterproofing for planters/podium.
- **Structure**: footing levels vs service trench depth (keep ≥ 1:1 influence line = trench not under footing zone; trench invert below footing needs concrete surround/sleeve); retaining walls; site retaining; basement excavation shoring/dewatering; ground slab level; duct banks below slabs.
- **MEP**: connection points (water, sewer, electrical, telecom, gas, DC) positions and levels at building face; pit/tank locations; roof-to-ground downpipe discharges; AC condensate; fuel tanks; generator exhaust/noise; substation access.
- **Fire**: fire truck route, hardstanding, hydrant positions, FDC, separation distances.
- **Landscape**: irrigation, trees roots vs services (min 2-3 m ⚠), planter drainage.

## 11. Pre-issue checklist
- Datum, units, coordinate system, benchmark and scale stated; north arrow; key plan.
- Boundary dims match deed/survey; setbacks dimensioned; coverage/FAR/parking calculated.
- All levels consistent with arch FFL; no negative gradients on pavements; slopes labelled with arrows; ramps ≤ code slope; accessible routes ≤ 5%/1:20, crossfall ≤ 2%.
- Drainage: pipe sizes, gradients, inverts continuous; manholes at changes; cover depth adequate; outfall level feasible (invert at outfall below upstream); no conflicts with footings, trees, other services.
- Utilities connection points confirmed by authority; meter rooms accessible; trench corridors sized and separated.
- Fire access dims (width, radius, clear height) and hydrants placed.
- Setting-out coordinates for all corners and control points; checked by diagonals; unit consistency.
- Parking dims, aisle, accessible count, ramp slopes/headrooms checked.
- Retaining walls designed/detailed; edge protection; erosion control; temporary works.
- Cross-check with architecture, structure, MEP composites; revision/issue log.
